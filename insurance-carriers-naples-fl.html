Auto Insurance Carriers

Auto

Bristol West

Naples, FL

Bristol West, part of the Farmers Insurance Group, specializes in non-standard auto coverage for Florida drivers who don't qualify for standard underwriting. That typically means a recent accident, a lapse in prior coverage, a prior cancellation, or a driving history that doesn't fit the criteria most carriers require. In Southwest Florida, Bristol West is one of the more consistent options for these profiles — they write SR-22 certificates for Naples-area clients who need proof of financial responsibility on file with the Florida DHSMV. I work with Bristol West because they accept the kinds of risk that standard carriers decline, and their pricing in the non-standard segment tends to be reasonable relative to what's available here in Collier and Lee County. If you've been turned down elsewhere, Bristol West is usually one of my first calls.

Bristol West specializes in non-standard auto insurance for drivers who don't qualify for standard coverage — prior accidents, coverage lapses, or SR-22 requirements. I use them regularly for Naples and SWFL clients with complex driving histories.

Yes. Bristol West files SR-22 certificates with the Florida DHSMV for drivers who need proof of financial responsibility. It's one of the key reasons I keep them in my lineup.

Auto · Specialty

Foremost Insurance

Southwest Florida

Foremost Insurance, a Berkshire Hathaway company, occupies a specific lane in the auto market: specialty vehicles and non-standard auto for drivers who fall outside standard criteria. That includes motorcycles, RVs, classic cars, and drivers with profiles that standard carriers won't touch cleanly. In Naples and across Southwest Florida, Foremost is especially useful for clients with a mixed portfolio — a clean daily driver who also needs to insure a seasonal vehicle or a motorcycle they take out on Gulf Coast roads. Their underwriting is more flexible than most standard carriers, and their financials are solid given the Berkshire backing. I place Foremost policies when a client's needs span multiple vehicle types or when their history calls for a carrier that evaluates the full picture rather than applying a rigid standard formula.

Yes. Foremost is a strong option for specialty vehicle coverage in SWFL — motorcycles, RVs, and classic cars alongside non-standard auto. I use them when a client has vehicles that don't fit a standard policy cleanly.

Yes. Foremost is part of Berkshire Hathaway, which gives them strong financial backing. They've been writing specialty and non-standard coverage in Florida for decades.

Auto

GEICO

Naples, FL

GEICO partners with independent agents across Florida, which means Naples drivers can access GEICO's competitive rates while working with a local agent who understands Southwest Florida's specific exposures — seasonal traffic surges, hurricane prep, and the elevated uninsured driver rate in this market. GEICO is a strong fit for drivers with clean or near-clean records who want the reliability of a recognized name without going through a call center. I write GEICO auto policies for clients across Naples, Marco Island, Estero, and Bonita Springs who want straightforward coverage, predictable pricing, and someone local to call when it actually matters. If your profile fits GEICO's underwriting criteria, it's consistently one of the most competitive options I can put in front of you.

Yes. GEICO works with licensed independent agents in Florida. When you get a GEICO policy through Signature Trust, you get GEICO's coverage with a local agent handling your account.

GEICO generally works best for drivers with clean or near-clean records. For profiles that fall outside standard criteria, I'd typically look at Bristol West, National General, or Sterling MGA first — I can compare across all of them in the same quote session.

It depends on your profile. As an independent agent I run GEICO alongside Foremost, National General, and Bristol West, so you see the real difference in writing rather than my opinion on it.

Auto

National General

Naples, FL

National General, now an Allstate company, occupies the space between standard and non-standard auto in Florida — the segment that's most commonly underserved. They write coverage for drivers who have some history that disqualifies them from the best standard rates but whose profiles don't warrant the full non-standard market either: a minor violation, a single at-fault accident, or a credit situation that puts them just outside the standard cutoff. For Naples and Southwest Florida clients, National General is often the right call when standard carriers decline or price too high but the non-standard segment feels disproportionate. They also carry solid roadside assistance and rental reimbursement options that matter for clients who commute regularly around the Naples area. I use them as a bridge carrier for clients who fall in that middle tier.

Yes. National General writes auto policies for drivers with minor violations or at-fault accidents who don't qualify for the best standard rates. They're one of my go-to options when standard carriers price too high.

They serve somewhat different profiles. GEICO works best for clean-record drivers; National General tends to make more sense when there's a recent violation or a single at-fault that pushes someone out of the standard tier.

Non-Standard Auto

Sterling MGA

Southwest Florida

Sterling MGA specializes in the non-standard auto segment for Florida drivers whose profiles place them outside what most carriers — including other non-standard options — will accept. That can mean multiple recent violations, an FR-44 requirement following a serious incident, a very short or interrupted licensing history, or a combination of factors that closes most doors. In Southwest Florida, where claim frequency in certain driving corridors is genuinely high, Sterling MGA fills a critical gap: clients who still need to be legally insured and financially protected even when their history is complicated. I place Sterling MGA policies for the hardest-to-place auto risks in the Naples area, typically after other non-standard options have also declined. If you've heard "no" more than once, this is where we look next.

Drivers with complex profiles who've been declined by standard and most non-standard carriers — multiple violations, FR-44 requirements, or other factors that close standard doors. Sterling MGA covers the segment that others typically won't.

An FR-44 is Florida's financial responsibility filing for certain serious violations, requiring higher liability limits than a standard SR-22. Sterling MGA can write policies with FR-44 filings for eligible drivers in Naples and SWFL.

Home Insurance Carriers

Homeowners

Slide Insurance

Naples, FL

Slide Insurance assumed more than 82,000 Citizens Insurance policies in 2023 — more than any other private carrier in Florida that year — and has continued writing actively across Collier and Lee County since. For Naples homeowners, that market presence matters at a moment when many national carriers have scaled back from Florida entirely. Slide offers solid wind and hurricane coverage, the two perils that drive most homeowners insurance decisions for properties in Southwest Florida, with pricing that stays competitive for homes meeting their underwriting criteria. I work with Slide for Naples-area clients who want a financially active, Florida-focused carrier — particularly those who've recently been dropped by a prior insurer, received a non-renewal, or are looking for a real alternative to Citizens. If you're shopping home coverage in Naples right now, Slide should be on the list.

Yes. Slide actively writes homeowners policies in Collier County and Lee County, including Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, and Cape Coral.

Slide took on more Citizens depopulation policies in 2023 than any other private carrier in Florida. For most Naples homeowners, Slide offers comparable or stronger wind/hurricane coverage with more responsive service — and without Citizens' special assessment exposure after a major storm.

Flood Insurance Carriers

Private Flood

Aon Edge

Naples, FL

Aon Edge is a private flood insurance product that functions as an alternative — or supplement — to the standard National Flood Insurance Program policy. For Naples and Southwest Florida homeowners, Aon Edge often provides broader coverage than the federal program: higher building and contents limits, coverage for additional living expenses after a flood event, and in certain flood zones, more competitive pricing than NFIP rates. The private flood market in Florida has expanded significantly since Hurricane Irma, and Aon Edge is one of the more established options in that space. I use Aon Edge for clients who need coverage beyond what NFIP's $250,000 building / $100,000 contents caps provide, who want living expense coverage included, or who are comparing private flood pricing against an upcoming NFIP renewal in a Naples or SWFL flood zone.

Yes. Aon Edge writes private flood policies in Collier County and across Southwest Florida. It's available as a standalone alternative or as a supplement to an existing NFIP policy.

Aon Edge typically offers higher limits, broader coverage terms, and living expense coverage that NFIP doesn't include. For some Naples flood zones, the private rate is also more competitive than the federal rate at renewal.

Private Flood

Neptune Flood

Southwest Florida

Neptune Flood uses property-level flood modeling to price risk more precisely than FEMA flood zone maps, which often work at the neighborhood level rather than the individual parcel. For Naples homeowners, that precision can translate to better pricing — properties that FEMA maps conservatively may receive more competitive rates from Neptune because the actual flood exposure of the specific site is lower than the zone classification implies. Neptune writes both residential and commercial flood coverage across Southwest Florida, with higher limits than NFIP, replacement cost coverage, and no waiting period in most cases when replacing an existing flood policy. I recommend Neptune to Naples-area clients who want competitive private flood pricing, faster processing than the federal program, and coverage terms that go further than what NFIP offers at renewal.

Yes. Neptune Flood writes private flood insurance in Collier County and across Southwest Florida, including Naples, Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Bonita Springs.

Neptune typically has no waiting period when replacing an existing flood policy. In most other cases it's shorter than NFIP's standard 30-day wait. I can confirm the specifics based on your situation.

NFIP · WYO

Wright Flood

Naples, FL

Wright Flood is one of the largest Write-Your-Own (WYO) carriers for the National Flood Insurance Program in the United States, administering NFIP-backed policies with private company service. For Naples homeowners who need or prefer federal NFIP coverage — whether for mortgage compliance, specific property requirements, or because the federal rate is the most competitive option available — Wright Flood delivers that coverage with more accessible service and claims handling than going through FEMA directly. They also offer excess flood coverage above NFIP limits for higher-value Naples properties where the federal cap leaves a gap. I work with Wright Flood for clients in NFIP-required situations and for those who want federal flood coverage administered by one of the most experienced WYO providers in the Florida market.

A Write-Your-Own (WYO) carrier like Wright Flood administers NFIP-backed federal flood policies under their own name. The coverage is federally backed, but you work with Wright Flood directly for billing, service, and claims — which tends to be significantly more responsive than FEMA directly.

Yes. Wright Flood writes NFIP policies across Florida, including high-risk flood zones in Naples and Collier County. They're one of my preferred WYO options for clients with mortgage-required flood coverage.
